Ascend Keys

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Short version: ASCEND Keys has no accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking. If you voluntarily join the news list, we store your email address only to send ASCEND Keys news and updates. Google Play handles subscription payments.

What the app collects

There is no user account, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or crash-reporting or tracking service built into the app. If you choose to join the optional news list, we collect the email address you submit and the time you consented.

The app stores one small piece of information locally on your device: whether you currently have an active subscription, so the app knows whether to show the member-only spreads. This value never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

News and updates

If you opt in through the About screen, your email address is stored securely in our Neon-hosted database and synchronized with Resend, our email-delivery provider. It is used only to send occasional ASCEND Keys news, new practices, and release updates. Signing up is optional and is based on your consent. We do not sell your address or use it for advertising profiles.

Payments

The optional monthly subscription is processed entirely through Google Play Billing. Google — not the developer of this app — collects and processes your payment information (such as payment method and billing details). We never see, receive, or store your payment details. Google's handling of that data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing — subscription purchases and renewals. Governed by Google's own privacy policy, linked above.
  • Neon — secure database hosting for the optional news-list email addresses.
  • Resend — management and delivery of optional news emails, including unsubscribe handling.

Children's privacy

ASCEND Keys is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, regardless of age.

Data retention and deletion

News-list email addresses are kept in Neon and Resend until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove yours. You can use the unsubscribe link in a news email or request deletion by emailing admytruk79@gmail.com from the subscribed address. Uninstalling the app removes the local subscription-status value stored on your device. To manage or cancel your subscription, use Google Play's own subscription management (Play Store → Menu → Subscriptions).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to admytruk79@gmail.com.
