Ascend Keys

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Short version: ASCEND Keys does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. The only third party involved is Google Play Billing, which handles subscription payments under Google's own privacy policy.

What the app collects

ASCEND Keys itself collects nothing. There is no sign-up, no user account, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no crash-reporting or tracking service built into the app.

The app stores one small piece of information locally on your device: whether you currently have an active subscription, so the app knows whether to show the member-only spreads. This value never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Payments

The optional monthly subscription is processed entirely through Google Play Billing. Google — not the developer of this app — collects and processes your payment information (such as payment method and billing details). We never see, receive, or store your payment details. Google's handling of that data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing — subscription purchases and renewals. Governed by Google's own privacy policy, linked above.

No other third-party services are used by the app at this time. If that changes in a future update (for example, an optional AI-assisted feature that sends user-provided text to a backend), this policy will be updated first to describe exactly what is sent and why, before that feature is enabled.

Children's privacy

ASCEND Keys is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, regardless of age.

Data retention and deletion

Because the app does not collect or transmit personal data, there is no server-side data to retain or delete. Uninstalling the app removes the local subscription-status value stored on your device. To manage or cancel your subscription, use Google Play's own subscription management (Play Store → Menu → Subscriptions).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date above.
