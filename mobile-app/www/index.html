How this works

This isn't divination — it's a structured self-reflection practice. Every card comes with a breath: a specific pattern to work with your body in the moment, not just words to read.

Each card offers two readings, kept deliberately separate. Grounded names a real psychological pattern happening now — not a prediction about the future. Spirit / Soul is the symbolic, poetic layer beneath it. You choose which register to sit with; neither is presented as the "real" answer over the other.

Every card also names a specific Tested Quality — not a vague theme, but the actual capacity this moment is asking of you.
